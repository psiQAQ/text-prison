# Scope and attribution

TextPrison is an original project prototype, not an ASD, Google, Microsoft, OpenAI, or Anthropic product or certification.
The package does not include the ASD-STE100 dictionary or copies of the complete source guides. Its compact instructions are original paraphrases and project-specific policy choices.
See `sources.json` for attribution, retrieval date, and access limitations. Review the relevant source terms before importing third-party rules, examples, dictionaries, or documents. A free download is not by itself a redistribution permission.
A project license has not been selected by the user; publication does not silently choose one or attribute third-party content to a repository license.
The five Git submodules are separately licensed research snapshots. Their LICENSE files remain with the upstream code; fixed commits and inspected license-file hashes are recorded in sources.json. They are excluded from the installed TextPrison Skill and are not executed as part of the current writing workflow.

The Vale configurations refer to Google v0.7.1 and Microsoft v0.15.1 release packages from the vale-cli organization. The repository distributes original configuration, two original link-text rules, and paraphrased guidance, not those third-party packages. A later user-authorized `vale sync` downloads the referenced packages under their own upstream license terms. Package versions, selected rules, source links, and the unverified release-asset boundary are documented in `skills/text-prison/vale/README.md`; no package license is assigned to TextPrison by implication.

Chinese guidance paraphrases selected sections of Microsoft's Simplified Chinese Localization Style Guide and community Chinese Copywriting Guidelines; their full texts are not redistributed. The AutoCorrect configuration is an original selection of public configuration keys, not a bundled copy of the tool. AutoCorrect and the Vercel Skills installer retain their own upstream terms. Source versions, inspected sections and limitations are recorded in `sources.json` and the installed Skill references.
