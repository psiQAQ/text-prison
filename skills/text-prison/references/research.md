# Research
Distinguish evidence, assumptions, inference, and recommendations. Preserve scope, numerical values, units, equations, uncertainty, negative findings, and limitations. Do not turn correlation into causation or possibility into certainty. Keep enough derivation to reproduce the conclusion. Lead with the result when appropriate, but do not flatten an argument into disconnected short sentences. Do not enforce STE sentence limits or unconditional active voice.

Keep citations attached to the claims they support. If the evidence is unavailable, identify which claim cannot be checked. The research Vale profile checks only link wording in Markdown; it does not check the argument, equations, claims or statistical interpretation.

Do not turn a failure to find a statistically significant difference into equality or equivalence. Preserve qualifications such as “cannot rule out” when restructuring a sentence; a smoother alternative is not sufficient reason to weaken the original evidence boundary.
